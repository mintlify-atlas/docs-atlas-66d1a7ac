**backtest-ollama-casual** is a measurement rig for the paid-signals industry, built on [backtest-kit](https://github.com/tripolskypetr/backtest-kit). An LLM (`gemma4:31b-cloud` via [json-inference](https://github.com/tripolskypetr/json-inference)) parses each Telegram signal seller's guidance — text **and** chart screenshots — the engine executes it within minutes of publication, and event studies over minute candles answer the question nobody in that industry wants asked: **where does the edge actually live, and does it survive the seller's own money management?**

## Key Findings

One month of replay across two channels with completely different formats produced three concrete results:

- **72% directional accuracy at 12–24h** (13/18 signals) with mean **+1.42% unleveraged** — but the first 15–60 minutes are slightly negative. The edge lives at the overnight horizon, not at entry.
- **The seller's own plan is mathematically ruinous.** TP4/TP5 hit rate: 0/16. Following five scale-out targets with a ~4.5% stop produces EV around **−3.5…−4% per trade unleveraged**. The advertised 13–25x leverage turns a single stop into −60%–100% of margin.
- **All realized profit came from exit engineering.** Level-ratchet trailing alone: **−0.31%** on the month. Adding a structural breakeven rule (stop to entry after 50% progress to TP1): **+3.37%**.

<Note>
  One month, 16–18 events per claim — hypothesis-grade, not proof. Every number is reproducible from `measure-items` + `candle-items` via the scripts shipped in `content/*/assets/`.
</Note>
